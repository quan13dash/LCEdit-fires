### For people who want to fast import in newer version (v7.8.2+), follow these steps:
- Download **mdmfirereadytouse.json**
- Open Top 50, go to tab "Import & Export Data"
- Hit "Choose file" in "Load Save (From Top 50 Save)" and browse for the .json file you already downloaded in first step
- Hit OK twice in the dialog. Hit "Cancel" in 1st dialog will CANCEL IMPORTING, in 2nd dialog will RESET MOST YOUR DATA!
- Last but optional, go to fire setting and adjust to fit with your cases, such as "Update fire icons every X update interval(s)" or change fires conditions, especially Ice effect.
